# SotonShip 2026: Event-wide rules

## Core idea
Judges reward a **clear user**, a **working core** and a **plausible way to be paid for** over technical ambition. A simple tool someone would **start using next month** beats an impressive prototype nobody would adopt.

## Basics
- **Teams of four.** **One track per team**: pick the one where the user and business case are strongest.
- **Hacking window:** Fri 16 Oct 20:00 → Sun 18 Oct 13:00.
- **You own what you build.**

## Common track rules
- Build the entry **during the event**, and create its repository **after hacking opens**. Open-source libraries, templates and pre-trained models are fine. Your own earlier projects are **not**.
- The README must state **which parts were AI-generated**, **what existed before the event** and **who helped**.
- Show **where each number comes from and when it was collected**. Never present cached data as live, or synthetic data as real.
- The README must list **every data source** with its **date and licence**.
- Keep **API keys and passwords out of the repo**.

## Deliverables
Submit on your team page at [SotonShip Live](https://live.sotonship.space/) **before 13:00 Sunday**.

| # | Deliverable | Spec |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Demo video | 1–3 min, product in use, **uploaded to YouTube**, link submitted |
| 2 | Summary | ~150 words: who the user is, their problem and why it matters, what the solution does, how it could scale |
| 3 | Repository | Link to the GitHub repo |

## Judging

| Criterion | Weight | What judges want to see |
|---|---|---|
| Problem and user | 20% | A specific need, credible evidence, an understanding of the people affected |
| AI at the core | 20% | A demonstrated AI capability, tested against a sensible baseline |
| Working product | 25% | An end-to-end task, reproducible tests, a demonstrated failure or handoff |
| Business and scale | 20% | A buyer, value proposition, realistic costs, differentiation, a plan for a second customer |
| Responsibility and trust | 15% | Measured risk reduction, honest trade-offs, data provenance, meaningful human control |

**Process:** On Sunday afternoon every team pitches in **VC for a Day**, where the audience and judges invest virtual money. The **top 3 per track** go to a final panel that decides the places. The "Judging & how to win" page on SotonShip Live has the pitch structure and the extra questions judges ask per track.
