When the bus is late

Four bus lines cross the city on the same four-ship Saturday. Passengers take a bus when it beats walking: mostly people with luggage heading for the station or the ferry. Every bus runs late by an amount drawn from a lateness profile, so late buses mean longer waits, fuller stops and people missing trains.
